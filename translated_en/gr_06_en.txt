Approval of Charge Allowance for Vacant Posts** There are a large number of vacant posts in the State Police. Therefore, the charge of these vacant posts is entrusted to a civil servant in addition to their duties. Applications for approval of charge allowance are submitted through the office of the Director General of Police and the Chief of Police. It has been observed that these applications are submitted with incomplete details or after a long delay. Consequently, obtaining necessary details and clarity from the office causes delays in the application and increases the workload. Therefore, it is informed that applications for charge allowance are to be submitted with the following details, after verification in accordance with the GCSA (Pay) Rules 2002 and the instructions of the Accounts Department dated 28/02/2000 and 08/01/2001. (1) Copy of the order regarding the appointment and relief of the employee. (2) Full details in the prescribed check-list. (3) Certificate from the competent authority regarding charge satisfaction.
