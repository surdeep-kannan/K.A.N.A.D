Gujarat Government** Home Department** Resolution No. V.P./BDS/102006/0-259(Gi)** Secretariat, Gandhinagar** Date: 15/09/2023** Letter from Additional Director General of Police (ATS and CI), Ahmedabad, dated 14/02/2023. Preamble:** Gujarat has a coastline of 1214.7 km, located close to the international maritime boundary and extremely sensitive from a security perspective. Activities such as the illegal trafficking of weapons, ammunition, and explosives like RDX, as well as the smuggling of prohibited items like gold and silver by pirates in the past, pose a threat to national security. To prevent such illegal activities and strengthen the country's security, the Central Government established the Coastal Security Scheme. Under CSS Phase-2, the Central Government approved 31 interceptor boats for marine police stations along the state's coastal districts. The State Government has established 12 marine police stations. Currently, since the Central Government has not yet provided the boats, there is a requirement to hire them on rent. Considering the letter from the Additional Director General of Police (ATS and CI), Ahmedabad, dated 14/02/2023, and the Central Government's decision not to provide the boats, the State Government had considered the matter of hiring 12 boats on rent. Resolution:** After careful consideration, the State Government approves the hiring of 12 interceptor boats (out of the 31 approved by the Central Government for coastal patrolling) within a limit of Rs. 2,64,00,000/- (Rupees Two Crore Sixty-Four Lakh) for patrolling, valid until 28/02/2024. (1) The State Government will execute the contract for hiring the boats through the current tendering process. (2) Fixed patrol hours (4 to 8 hours per day) will be set, and a penalty clause will be included in the agreement for the relevant company if the boat does not patrol for the scheduled hours. (3) The 12 boats will be allocated for patrolling in more sensitive areas of coastal security. (4) This cost includes salaries for crew members and expenses for boat repairs and maintenance. (5) Annexure-A is attached regarding the specific specifications of the boats. The expenditure for this will be accounted for under Head 4320550080010 (MEP 27, Coastal Security) and will be met from the allocation made in the current year, or supplementary demand will be raised if necessary. This resolution is issued in accordance with the approval from the Finance Department's letter dated 24/08/2023. Issued in the name of and by the order of the Governor of Gujarat.
